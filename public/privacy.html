Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ReceiptPilot ("we", "us") is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME]. This policy explains what we collect when you use ReceiptPilot, why, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what's needed to run the app, we don't sell your data, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely as a hash by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile from Google.
  • Profile settings: the name you choose to display, your default currency, and any custom categories you create.
  • Receipts: the images and PDFs you upload, and the details read from them or entered by you (vendor, date, amounts, category, payment method such as card brand and last four digits, notes).
  • Usage counts: for example how many receipts were read in a month, used to run plan limits.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers may log standard information such as IP address, browser type and request times for security and reliability.

We don't use advertising trackers or sell analytics data.

How receipts are read

By default, receipts are read on your own device: the text recognition runs in your browser, and the image is then stored in your private account. If a paid plan with AI reading is offered and you choose it, receipt images are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to extract the receipt details for you.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app: store, display, organize and export your receipts.
  • To keep your account secure and the service reliable.
  • To process payments if you subscribe to a paid plan.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes (not marketing, unless you opt in).

Who processes your data

We use trusted service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Stripe: only if you subscribe; card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Anthropic: only if you use an AI reading feature, as described above.

We may disclose information if required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Who can see your receipts

Your receipts are stored in a private account. Database rules ensure each user can access only their own data, and images are only shown through short-lived private links.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), your receipts, images and profile are deleted. Copies in our providers' backups may remain for a limited period before being overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: download all your receipts and a CSV at any time from the Export page.
  • Correct: edit any receipt or setting in the app.
  • Delete: delete individual receipts, or your whole account, in the app.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have additional rights. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

ReceiptPilot is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we make important changes we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app or by email where appropriate.
